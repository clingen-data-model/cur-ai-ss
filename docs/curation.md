# Curation: what curators see, field notes, sign-off

Reach for this when changing curator-facing wording, the patient or
occurrence fields, or the sign-off and review flows.

## What curators see

Curators see "Individual" wherever the code says patient (since 2026-11-06): visible strings, table headers, API error details, API routes (`/papers/{id}/individuals`) and the functions behind them say Individual, while models, API fields (`patient_id`), tables and component names keep patient. The three task types named for the step were renamed on 2026-11-12 (Individual Extraction, Individual Demographics, Individual Variant Occurrences; their agents, handlers and output models with them; migration `d5f1b3a7c9e2`, which rewrites the stored names in `tasks` and `flags`). Curators never see a task type's value: each step has a plain-language name, `TaskType.label` ("Finding variants" for Variant Extraction), served by `GET /task-types` and shown through `taskLabel` (`frontend/src/lib/taskLabel.ts`, registered by the root layout before signed-in pages render); the paper chat is given both names and answers with the label, and snapshot and busy messages use it. Requests, logs and code keep the value. The progress timeline groups steps into the stages of `lib/tasks/tracks.py`, whose labels are curator-facing too

## Field notes

- **Patient** (`lib/models/patient.py`): A patient a pedigree places is identified position first, then the paper's label in parentheses ("II-1 (Subject 1)"), the format curators asked for on 2026-10-29 after naming came out mixed within one family; a patient no pedigree places keeps the paper's label alone. The identifier rules in `lib/agents/individual_extraction_agent.py` still decide which label the paper gives; this only fixes its form. The family is never in the identifier: it is `family_identifier`, two families may each have a "II-1" or a "sister", so a flag's `about` names every patient as family then identifier, "Family 2 II-1" (`individual_label` in `lib/tasks/flags.py`) and `PatientIdCell` shows the family beside the name when the paper has more than one Origin is three fields: `country_of_origin` is where the paper places the family (an enum of countries), `ancestry` the descent in the paper's own words (free text, nullable, migration `b8c3d7e2f4a1`, since 2026-10-29), `race` the census bucket that descent maps to; a "Brazilian family of Italian descent" had been landing in the country field as Italy for some members and Brazil for others. Four ages: `age_diagnosis` (the age the paper calls a diagnosis), `age_report` (age when described), `age_death`, and `age_onset` with `onset_category`, HPO's onset bucket (`OnsetCategory`: Antenatal ... Adult; migration `c9d4e8f3a5b2`, since 2026-10-29), because papers give onset far more often than diagnosis and the agent had been writing it into `age_diagnosis` for some patients and not others
- **PatientVariantOccurrence** (`lib/models/patient_variant_occurrences.py`): Links patients to variants with zygosity, testing info and two distinct inheritance facts: `inheritance` is the *mechanism* (Dominant, Recessive, ..., the same enum as the paper's `disease_inheritance_mode`; labelled "Inheritance mechanism" in the UI) and `variant_inheritance` is which parent the copy came from (Maternal, Paternal, Both parents, De novo, ...). They were one field until 2026-10-26, when the agent was found reading a paper's "Inheritance: Maternal" table cell as a mechanism; `variant_inheritance` replaced the `de_novo` boolean (migration `a4e7c2d9f1b3`), the UI exposes it only as the De Novo switch (on writes De novo, off writes Unknown; the evidence popover shows the full value), and rows from before the migration carry Unknown until Individual Variant Occurrences is re-run (`lib.bin.requeue_papers --all --from-task 'Individual Variant Occurrences'`)

## Curator sign-off and Review Mode

a stamp on each patient-variant occurrence (`reviewed_at`, `reviewed_by_user_id`; `lib/models/signoff.py`, migration `e5f1a7c3d9b8`, since 2026-11-03) says a curator checked it. The occurrence is the only stamped row: it is what curation checks, and stamping its patient and variant too asked for three confirmations of one fact. `POST /papers/{id}/signoff` stamps or unstamps a list of occurrences and then `sync_review_status` moves the paper's review status to match: first stamp on an unstarted paper, In progress under the curator who stamped; last occurrence stamped, Completed; a take-back on a completed paper, In progress again, assignee kept. Open flags never hold completion back. An agent rewriting an occurrence in place (compound-het pairing, MONDO on an occurrence) clears its stamp (`clear_signoff`) and reopens a completed paper; occurrence extraction recreates its rows, so the stamp goes with them; demographics, harmonization and annotation re-runs leave it. Review Mode (`/papers/$id/review`, the "Review Mode" button on the paper page, `GET /papers/{id}/review-cases` built by `lib/misc/review_cases.py`) walks the paper one family at a time in the paper's order: each case is the family's members, occurrences, variants and the open flags about any of them, shown with the paper page's own tables (`OccurrencesTab`, `UnassociatedPatientsTab`, `FamiliesTab` and `FlagsTab` take an `only` filter for this) beside the PDF with every citation the case carries highlighted; a detail row's View in PDF icon narrows the highlight to that field, "Highlight all case fields" widens it back. The compact occurrences table has no column for testing methods, disease name or MONDO, so its caret opens an Occurrence panel holding them (`OccurrenceDetailPanel.tsx`); the Patient and Variant panels are behind the names. Confirm stamps the family's occurrences and moves to the next unconfirmed family. A family no occurrence links, variants linked to nobody, patients with no linked variant and flags about nothing extracted are not cases: the walk is the occurrences alone, and the paper page's tabs keep the rest. Progress (`PaperResp.signoff`, stamped/total occurrences) shows on the paper page button and in the papers table. A paper's review can be assigned to several curators (`paper_review_assignees`, migration `f6a2b8d4c1e9`, since 2026-11-10; `PaperResp.review_assignees`, `PATCH /papers/{id}/review` takes the whole set as `assignee_user_ids`): the status stays one per paper, shared by everyone on it, and the dashboard's assignee filter matches any of them. `papers.review_assignee_user_id`, the single assignee it replaced, is still a mapped column that nothing reads or writes, because dropping it means rebuilding `papers`. Phenotypes carry the same stamp (migration `f7c2a9e4b1d6`, since 2026-11-19), written by the HPO review: the **Review HPO terms** button above an individual's phenotype table opens a modal (`HpoReviewDialog.tsx`) that walks the terms in paper order (each at its first cited passage, `anchorPosition` on the PDF context) beside the PDF, with keys `j`/`k` previous/next, `a` accept (`POST /papers/{id}/phenotypes/signoff`), `r` relink, `x` reject. Reject deletes the phenotype, like every other delete (it reaches `deletion_log`); opening the review snapshots the paper ("Before HPO review: <individual>") and that snapshot is the undo. A phenotype stamp does not move the paper's review status, and relinking, by a curator or the HPO agent, clears it.
